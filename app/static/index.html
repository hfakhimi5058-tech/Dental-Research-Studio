Methods & limitations

Research status

This is research software. The public ToothSeg model provides actual local tooth predictions. Our separate experimental training is unfinished. Pilot accuracy results are shown under Models & jobs; no clinical validation is claimed. Synthetic demonstrations are explicitly labelled.

Geometry

Volume = labelled voxel count × |det(A)|, where A is the spatial affine in millimetres. Bounds include entire voxel cells in source RAS coordinates. Distances transform both selected voxel centres into physical space before calculation.

Model evidence

Training data, label mappings, patient overlap and independent evaluation must be reviewed. A successful process or a plausible image does not demonstrate accuracy.

Local storage

Processing runs on loopback. The app uses no cloud inference or external asset CDN. Files stay in the project directory. A cloud-synchronized directory may copy files through its sync provider. Original source files are preserved. Browser downloads may be saved separately.

Removal

Use Stop.cmd, then delete this project folder. This chat, browser records, downloaded exports and OneDrive copies are separate records.
